# Privacy Policy for Padikkam

**Effective Date:** August 11, 2026

## 1. Introduction
Welcome to **Padikkam**. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your data when you use our mobile application. We built Padikkam with a strong commitment to privacy, simplicity, and offline accessibility. 

The core philosophy of Padikkam is that your data belongs to you. **We do not collect, store, or transmit your personal information to any external servers.**

## 2. Data Collection & Storage
Padikkam is designed to be a "100% Offline" app. 
* **Your Name:** The app asks for a display name to personalize your experience. This name is stored **strictly locally** on your device using an offline database. It is never sent to us or any third party.
* **Exam Progress & Streaks:** All your exam attempts, paused exams, scores, and study streaks are saved entirely on your device's local storage.
* **Telemetry & Tracking:** We do not use any analytics trackers, crash reporters, or advertising SDKs. We do not track how you use the app.

## 3. Internet Usage
The app requires an internet connection for one specific feature:
* **Downloading Exam Papers:** To keep the app size small, Padikkam connects to a public GitHub repository to download the latest KEAM exam question papers and manifest files. 
* During this sync process, **no user data, usage statistics, or device identifiers are sent**. The app simply requests the exam files from GitHub.

## 4. Data Security
Because all your personal data (name, progress, scores) remains on your physical device, its security is tied to the security of your device itself. If you uninstall the app or clear its data, your progress will be permanently deleted unless you have made a local backup.

## 5. Third-Party Services
We do not share any data with third-party services. The app downloads open-source question banks hosted on GitHub, which operates under its own privacy policies regarding basic network requests. 

## 6. Children's Privacy
Padikkam is an educational tool designed for students preparing for the KEAM entrance exam. Because we do not collect any personal data or require account registration, the app is completely safe for children under 13 and complies with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA).

## 7. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected in this document with an updated "Effective Date." Since we do not collect your contact information, we recommend checking this page periodically for updates.

## 8. Contact Us
If you have any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy or how Padikkam handles your data, please contact the developer via the app's official repository or support channels.
